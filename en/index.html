What it is

Built by riders, for riders

MOTOCLUBes brings together in one place the three things that today live in five different apps: planning the route, navigating it on a bike, and riding it with your people.

Plan

Build the route by curves, back roads or toll-free. Add fuel, food and hotel stops. Import the GPX files you already have.

Navigate

GPS navigation designed for motorcycles: screen stays on, keeps running in the background, voice ready for your headset.

Ride together

Create the group, publish the ride, and out on the road everyone sees each other on the map and talks over the built-in intercom.

Your routes

Multi-day planner

Stages with their own colour, distance and time calculated, and the whole ride summed up on one map.

Import, edit and export GPX

Not just view them: bring your GPX in, reshape it as you like and take it out again whenever you want.

Why it exists

The route, the group and the voice are never in the same place

Anyone who rides knows it: the route in one app, the meet-up in a WhatsApp group, the voice in an intercom that will not talk to the one next to it, and halfway there nobody knows where the rest are. Five tools for a single ride.

That is where MOTOCLUBes comes from. Not one more app, but planning the route, navigating it and riding it with your people no longer being three separate things. Riders are building it, it is tested on the road, and we are still at it.

FAQ

What you keep asking us

When can I download it?

It is finishing its road-testing phase with real riders. As soon as it is published on the App Store and Google Play, both links will appear on this very page.

Do I need a 400 euro intercom?

No. Group voice runs over your phone's data, so any Bluetooth headset or intercom you already have in your helmet will do, whatever the brand. And it does not depend on the device's range: as long as there is signal, you keep talking.

Does it work with the phone stowed and the screen locked?

Yes. Navigation keeps running in the background with the GPS active, which is exactly what you need when the phone is in your pocket or on the mount with the screen off.

Can I bring the routes I already have?

Yes. Import your existing GPX files and export the new ones as GPX whenever you want. Your routes are yours and they leave the same way they came in.

Can everyone see where I am?

Not by default. Live tracking only runs during a ride, it has to be switched on for the group, and on top of that every rider has their own switch in their profile to stop sharing their position even if the group has it enabled.

Is this only for clubs with patches and a hierarchy?

No. It works just as well for a club with its own structure as for four friends going out for breakfast on a Sunday. A group is simply the people you ride with.

What if my group mixes iPhone and Android?

It makes no difference. The app is native on both platforms and everything works across them: the group, the ride, the map and the voice.

Who is behind this?

MOTOCLUBES TECH S.L., a company based in Barcelona. Our own product, our own infrastructure and continuous development. We are nobody's side project.
