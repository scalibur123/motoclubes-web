What it is

Built by riders, for riders

MOTOCLUBes brings together in one place the three things that today live in five different apps: planning the route, navigating it on a bike, and riding it with your people.

🗺️

Plan

Build the route by curves, back roads or toll-free. Add fuel, food and hotel stops. Import the GPX files you already have.

🧭

Navigate

GPS navigation designed for motorcycles: screen stays on, keeps running in the background, voice ready for your headset.

👥

Ride together

Create the group, publish the ride, and out on the road everyone sees each other on the map and talks over the built-in intercom.

Features

Everything you can do

What is already built and working in the app, and what comes next.

🛣️

Route planner

Multi-day rides, colour-coded stages, distance and time calculated.

📥

GPX import and export

Bring in the routes you already have and take the new ones anywhere.

🧭

Motorcycle navigation

Background mode, screen awake and continuous GPS even with the phone locked.

Group intercom

Voice between the riders on the trip, with nothing else to install.

Live tracking
